VR & AR Technology Overview

🥽 Virtual Reality (VR)

Virtual Reality creates a fully immersive digital environment using VR headsets. Players experience games as if they are inside the virtual world.

📱 Augmented Reality (AR)

Augmented Reality overlays digital objects onto the real world through smartphones or AR glasses, blending virtual content with reality.

🎯 Benefits

• Immersive Gameplay
• Motion Tracking
• Realistic Interaction
• Better Learning Experience
